Hands-free computer control

Your head is the mouse.

The problem

Millions can't use a mouse
or keyboard.

Who

People with spinal cord injury, ALS, cerebral palsy, amputation, stroke, or severe RSI.

Today's options

Commercial head mice and eye trackers are expensive and hard to set up without help.

Free tools

Webcam trackers depend on lighting, drain the laptop, and still need a way to click and type.

Result: losing hand movement often means losing independence on a computer: study, work, communication.

The solution

A headband that is a mouse,
and a voice that clicks.

Headband

ESP32 + MPU6050 on a foam headband, 1200 mAh battery. A real Bluetooth mouse: tilt toward a shoulder for left/right, lift or lower the chin for up/down. Works the moment it pairs.

Windows app

30+ offline voice commands, voice typing, spoken onboarding, guided calibration, 7 practice lessons and personal profiles. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Click, double, right, middle click
  • Scroll, drag, back / forward
  • Voice typing with punctuation
  • Pause and emergency stop
How it works

From head tilt to cursor in 15 ms.

MPU6050

measures head tilt

→

ESP32

personal movement model: dead zone, curve, speed

→

Bluetooth HID

standard mouse reports every 15 ms

→

Windows

cursor moves

Any mounting angle

The firmware learns each user's straight-ahead position; calibration checks the head is still and upright.

Hardware clicks

Voice clicks are sent by the headband itself, so they even work on the On-Screen Keyboard.

Offline speech

Vosk for commands, faster-whisper for typing, all on the user's own computer.
