What it does
A low-cost head-controlled mouse for people with upper limb disability. Tilt your head to move the cursor; speak to click, scroll and type.
How it works
ESP32 + MPU6050 headband acting as a Bluetooth mouse with a personal movement model. Offline voice: Vosk for commands, Whisper for typing.
Privacy
Everything runs on this computer. No internet connection is needed and nothing is uploaded.
