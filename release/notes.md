## iShara 2.0.0 for Windows

**One complete offline installer**: the app, its runtime and all three speech models. No Python, no extra downloads, no account.

- Guided first-run setup: microphone, headband, Bluetooth, training, calibration (training and calibration can be skipped)
- Calibration detects how the headband sits on your head automatically
- Voice clicks work everywhere, including the On-Screen Keyboard
- Hand mouse mode, middle click, back and forward buttons
- Help page with read-aloud guides; check for updates in About
- Your profiles and calibration are kept when you update

**Install:** download `iShara-Setup-2.0.0.exe`, open it, press Install. Windows 10 (1809+) or 11, 64-bit.
